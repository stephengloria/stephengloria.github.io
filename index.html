Modification change tracking

Specs, edge cases, testing

Problem
Government contracts are modified many times after award. Customers compared versions by hand to see what changed.
What I did
Wrote the requirements and acceptance criteria for tracking changes across the whole modification chain, down to each field on each line item, including lines added, removed, renumbered or changed twice.
Outcome
Shipped across successive 2026 releases, tested against real contracts before each one.

AI extraction, prototype to customer-ready

Discovery through rollout

Problem
The extraction engine worked as a developer prototype, but nobody had defined what correct output looked like for a customer.
What I did
Defined the structured output for every award form, line item and clause section, validated it on real awards and wrote the documentation customers evaluate it with.
Outcome
Went from an internal demo to something customers test with their own documents.

SAP and Salesforce integrations

ERP and CRM, APIs, acceptance testing

Problem
Contract data had to move cleanly between the product and the systems customers already run their business on.
What I did
Led the integration requirements with engineering and customer IT: field mapping, data flows to the ERP or CRM, and acceptance testing against customer data.
Outcome
A defined, tested path for contract data in and out of the customer's ERP and CRM.

From escalations to a reference account

Customer feedback into fixes

Problem
Viasat, a satellite communications customer, was raising friction points faster than they were being closed.
What I did
Logged every issue with screenshots and video, drove fixes into the next release and handed the rest to engineering as tickets they could reproduce first try.
Outcome
The account moved from escalations to a reference.
